Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a username. If you sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email; we receive only the relay address Apple provides.
  • Profile details you choose to add: display name, photo, bio, location, favorite genres.
  • Your collection: the records, films, and books you catalog, play or watch counts, ratings, notes, shelves, want list, and mood mixes.
  • Social activity, if you opt in: who you follow, what you post to the Feed and The Lobby, and reactions. Social features are 13+, and we ask for a date of birth before turning them on.
  • Diagnostics and usage: anonymous screen views and feature counts that help us see what is used, plus crash information.

How we use it

  • To sync your collection across your devices and keep it backed up.
  • To generate features from your own data, such as Mood Mixes, Tonight's Pick, Insights, and your Taste DNA portrait. Portrait text is generated by a model that receives a summary of your collection and returns text; it is not used to train anything.
  • To power the social features you opt into.
  • To send notifications you have turned on. You can turn each type off in Settings → Notifications.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your data with third parties for their marketing.
  • We do not show advertising.

Third-party services

StackRoom is built on Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, storage, and push notifications; on Discogs for release data and, if you import, your public Discogs collection; on TMDB and Google Books for film and book metadata; and on Apple's StoreKit for subscriptions. Each has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

  • Export your collection at any time from the You tab (Export Collection).
  • Delete your account and all associated data from You → Settings → Privacy → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Contact us at hello@getstackroom.com to access or correct your data.

Children

StackRoom is not directed at children under 13. Social features require a verified age of 13 or older.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will note it in the app and update the date above.
